===============================================================================
3. PROCESSING PIPELINE (per well)
===============================================================================

Each well's raw time series goes through 5 steps:

Step 1 — SPIKE REMOVAL (first pass, threshold_mult=5.0)
  - Compute median absolute step size across the time series
  - For each interior point, compare deviation from neighbor average
  - Mark as spike if: deviation > 5x median step AND > 2x neighbor difference
  - Replace spikes with NaN, then linearly interpolate across them

Step 2 — NaN GAP CHECK (MAX_NAN_GAP=7)
  - If any consecutive NaN run exceeds 7 timestamps, EXCLUDE the well entirely
  - Otherwise, linearly interpolate to fill all NaN gaps

Step 3 — SPIKE REMOVAL (second pass, threshold_mult=2.5)
  - More aggressive — catches smaller spikes missed in step 1
  - Same algorithm, lower threshold

Step 4 — FINAL NaN FILL
  - Any remaining NaN filled by linear interpolation
  - Wells with fewer than 4 valid points excluded entirely

Step 5 — INTENSIVE SMOOTHING
  a. LOWESS pass 1: frac=0.55 (very broad, captures overall trend)
  b. LOWESS pass 2: frac=0.45 (moderate)
  c. LOWESS pass 3: frac=0.35 (narrower, refines shape)
  d. Gaussian filter: sigma=3.0 (removes residual jitter)
  e. CubicSpline: bc_type=((1, 0.0), (2, 0.0))
     - Left boundary: clamped (zero slope at t=0, flat baseline start)
     - Right boundary: natural (free curvature)
  f. Evaluate on 1000-point fine grid for smooth output curves


===============================================================================
4. AVERAGING AND OUTLIER BASELINE SHIFT (averaged plot)
===============================================================================

The averaged plot uses raw averaging with a targeted shift for the two
most extreme baselines:

  1. Process all 22 wells through the smoothing pipeline
  2. Average the smoothed traces per concentration (no per-well offset)
  3. Identify the concentration with the HIGHEST average start value
     and the concentration with the LOWEST average start value
  4. Shift only those two traces (uniform vertical shift of the entire
     trace, not just the starting point):

     HIGHEST (12 mM):
       - Raw average start: 27.2% O2
       - Second-highest start: 18.5% (1.5 mM)
       - Shifted DOWN by 7.2% so it starts at 20.0%
         (1.5% margin above the second-highest)

     LOWEST (0.094 mM):
       - Raw average start: 5.7% O2
       - Second-lowest start: 11.5% (6 mM)
       - Shifted UP by 4.3% so it starts at 10.0%
         (1.5% margin below the second-lowest)

  5. All other concentrations (6, 3, 1.5, 0.75, 0.38, 0.19 mM) are
     plotted at their raw averaged values with no adjustment

     MARGIN parameter = 1.5% (gap preserved between shifted trace and
     its nearest neighbor)

  WHY this approach:
  - The 12 mM group's high baseline was driven by one remaining well
    (well 0) starting at 41% O2, pulling the 2-well average up
  - The 0.094 mM group has only 1 well starting at 5.7%, creating
    an artificially large gap from the pack (11–19%)
  - A uniform shift preserves the shape and magnitude of the response
    while closing the gap — the relative change over time is unchanged
  - Unlike full offset/normalization, this keeps the middle 6
    concentrations at their true measured values

===============================================================================
8. DESIGN DECISIONS AND ALTERNATIVES CONSIDERED
===============================================================================

Three baseline-handling approaches were tested before settling on this one:

  a. COMPRESS=0.0 (all traces start at identical point)
     - Clean fan-out but looks artificial — all lines converge to one dot
     - Rejected: user preferred some natural spread

  b. No offset at all (raw averages)
     - Natural but 12 mM starts at 27% while 6 mM starts at 11%
     - The 12 mM baseline is misleadingly high due to one high-baseline well
     - 0.094 mM is isolated far below the pack at 5.7%
     - Rejected: too much visual gap between outliers and the main group

  c. COMPRESS=0.3 (30% of original spread preserved)
     - Intermediate approach, tested first
     - Rejected: baselines still too bunched together for this dataset

  d. Targeted shift of only the two extreme traces (CHOSEN)
     - Preserves all middle concentrations at true values
     - Only adjusts the two traces whose baselines were dominated by
       well-level artifacts (high-baseline well in 12 mM, single low
       well in 0.094 mM)
     - Uniform shift keeps response shape intact
